About cliphou.se

You ship it. Cliphouse shows it off. Your coding agent turns every release into a video for your socials. It finds the selling points, picks the angle, writes the copy, and uses your brand colors, so a small team without a marketer still ships a polished video. It works in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Claude, and ChatGPT, and the templates are free to edit with no watermark or attribution required.

Who it is for

Founders, marketers, creators, and developers who need launch videos, social ads, reels, event promos, or recap videos without starting in a timeline editor.

How it works

  1. Browse templates by purpose, format, style, energy, tone, and scene.
  2. Open a video and click its text to edit it.
  3. Choose a palette or apply your brand kit.
  4. Save, share, hand off to a coding agent, or download the source.

Frequently asked questions

How is cliphou.se different from Canva or CapCut?

Canva and CapCut are broad editing tools. cliphou.se focuses on motion graphics templates that are generated as code, so the gallery can offer bespoke layouts and timing while keeping text and color edits simple.

Why not ask Claude or ChatGPT to make a video from scratch?

A model can write motion graphics code, but you still need a starting idea, a working render setup, and edit tools. cliphou.se lets you compare finished results before you start editing.

Are the templates built with Remotion?

Yes. Templates are React and Remotion compositions. Each template uses shared text roles and five theme colors, so the editor and brand kit work across the gallery.

Do I need an account?

Not to start. You can browse and edit up to 3 templates without an account. A free Google sign-in unlocks unlimited editing, MP4 downloads, starred videos, and private saved edits.

Can I export an MP4?

Every video downloads as a standalone Remotion project with your template version, exact dependencies, and a one-line render command. Hosted publishing creates share links with preview images when the sharing service is enabled.
